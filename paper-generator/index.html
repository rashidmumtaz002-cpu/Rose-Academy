How to use

  1. Generate Paper: pick class, subject and chapters → choose how many MCQs, short and long questions → tick questions yourself or press Random Select → write paper name, date and time → print.
  2. Question Bank: see all textbook questions, and add your own questions any time — they join the same bank.
  3. Equations: in your own questions write frac{1}{2} for a fraction, sqrt{2} for √2, x^2 for x² — they print as proper mathematics.
  4. Print: on the paper preview, use Print Paper for the question paper, Print with Answer Key for the teacher copy, and turn on the Bubble Sheet when you want the OMR answer sheet with it.
