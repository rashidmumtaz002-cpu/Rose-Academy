Paper Settings — MS Word style: the body of the paper; it applies to every preview and print, and each chapter prints under its own heading. The header name, colours and font style are set in Account Settings above — its colour is also the heading-bar colour.

How to use

  1. Generate Paper: pick class, subject and chapters → choose the medium (English Medium, Urdu Medium or Dual Medium) and how many MCQs, short and long questions → tick questions yourself or press Random Select → write paper name, date and time → print.
  2. Question Bank: see all textbook questions, and add your own questions any time — they join the same bank.
  3. Equations: in your own questions write frac{1}{2} for a fraction, sqrt{2} for √2, x^2 for x² — they print as proper mathematics.
  4. Print: on the paper preview, use Print Paper for the question paper, Print with Answer Key for the teacher copy, and turn on the Bubble Sheet when you want the OMR answer sheet with it.
  5. Paper Settings: set the paper like MS Word — font, font size, line spacing, page margins, space between questions and heading colour — in Backup & Settings, or above the paper preview in the last step of Generate Paper. In the paper, questions print chapter-wise: every chapter gets its own heading.
